CAPSTONE THREE: MODEL METRICS
Investigating Fatigue-Related Aviation Safety Reports Through Statistical Analysis and NLP
Author: Brittany Barrion| Springboard Data Science Career Track

=====================================================================
1. DATA
=====================================================================
Source:            NASA Aviation Safety Reporting System (ASRS) Database Online
Sample:            2,191 fatigue-related reports, 2009-2025
Model input:       Lemmatized primary narrative (text_lemma)
Train / test:      1,752 / 439 reports (80/20), stratified by flight phase, random_state = 42

=====================================================================
2. FINAL MODEL: NON-NEGATIVE MATRIX FACTORIZATION (NMF)
=====================================================================
Library:           scikit-learn 1.6.1 (sklearn.decomposition.NMF)

Hyperparameters
  n_components (topics)   8      (tuned over 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14)
  init                    nndsvda
  max_iter                1000
  random_state            42

Features (TF-IDF vectorizer, fit on training narratives only)
  ngram_range             (1, 2)   single words + two-word phrases
  min_df                  5        word must appear in >= 5 narratives
  max_df                  0.5      word must appear in <= 50% of narratives
  sublinear_tf            True
  norm                    L2
  custom stop words       u, us, back, could, also, get, got, one, due, told, asked,
                          said, gave, would, went, going, way, even, still, made,
                          make, sure, know, think, felt (plus NLTK English list)
  vocabulary size         9,925 terms (5,660 two-word phrases)

Performance metrics
  Topic coherence, NPMI (test set)        0.221   (primary metric; range -1 to 1)
  Topic coherence, NPMI (training set)    0.243   (small gap = generalizes)
  Topic diversity (unique top-25 words)   0.875
  Stability across random seeds           1.000   (Jaccard of top-10 words)
  Reconstruction error per report         0.959 train / 0.961 test
